You run a corner shop that sells coffee, bagels, and juice. A two-week season that starts with little cash, so stock must be bought a day at a time.

Each day, read the books, buy stock from the supplier, set prices if you like, then
open the shop with `wait`. The season lasts 14 days. Make as much money as you can,
and never let cash go below zero: a purchase is paid at once, and rent is due at the end
of every day.
