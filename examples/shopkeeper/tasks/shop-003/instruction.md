You run a corner shop that sells coffee, bagels, and juice. A three-week season with higher rent and customers who mind prices less.

Each day, read the books, buy stock from the supplier, set prices if you like, then
open the shop with `wait`. The season lasts 21 days. Make as much money as you can,
and never let cash go below zero: a purchase is paid at once, and rent is due at the end
of every day.
