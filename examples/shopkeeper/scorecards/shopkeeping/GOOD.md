# Shopkeeping: what good means

Owner: The shop's owner

A good season never dips below zero cash, not even for a moment after a big
delivery, and ends with more cash than it started with.

## Must pass

- **never_negative_cash**

## Aim for

- **cash_on_hand** (>= 400): Cash at the end of the season.

## Keep an eye on

- **turns** (<= 200)
