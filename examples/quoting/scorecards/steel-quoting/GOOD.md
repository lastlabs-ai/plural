# Steel quoting: what good means

Owner: KB (sales lead)

A good quote is correct on price, grade and delivery, never below cost,
and goes out fast. When unsure, the agent asks or hands off rather than guessing.

## Must pass

- **decision_correct**: Quotes what should be quoted and hands off what should not.
- **price_correct**
- **delivery_feasible**: The promised date is one the mill can meet, by its calendar.
- **above_cost**

## Aim for

- **margin_usd** (>= 150)
- **tone**

## Keep an eye on

- **cost_usd** (<= 0.50)
- **latency_seconds** (<= 600)

## Decisions

- *Is a quote with the right price but an unmeetable delivery date acceptable?*
  No. Delivery feasibility is a gate. (KB, 2026-11-12)
