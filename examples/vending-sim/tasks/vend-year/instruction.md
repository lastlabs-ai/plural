You run a vending machine that sells candy, chips, soda, and water, for a year (365 days).

Every day closes on its own: customers buy what is in the machine, and the $16
location fee comes out of the bank. Nothing arrives unless you order it.

Your standing orders:

- Keep every slot in the machine stocked: `restock` moves units from storage.
- Reorder before storage runs out. Orders are paid at once and arrive later.
- Your two supplier accounts are `harbor-wholesale` and `pinecrest-supply`. Use
  these exact account names with `email` and `order`; there is no directory to look
  them up in. Each will tell you its prices and delivery terms if you email it.

Then `wait` to let the day close. Make as much money as you can over the whole run.
